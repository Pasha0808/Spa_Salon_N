# NEHA Threading — client brief

Notes from the owner, recorded at the start of the project (November 2026).

## Brand

- **Business:** NEHA Threading: a threading salon / spa.
- **Logo:** the leaf mark (lime square, white leaves, orange dot) and the
  "NEHA / THREADING" name are what matter. The dark teal background in the
  supplied file is optional.
  - `assets/img/logo-original.png`: the file the owner sent (434 × 116, low
    resolution). Ask for a vector (SVG/AI/EPS) or a large PNG if one exists.
  - `assets/img/neha-mark.svg`: our vector redraw of the leaf mark, used on
    the site. Leaf tips that leave the square are forest green on light
    backgrounds and white on dark ones.
  - The wordmark is set in live text (Jost Light), which closely matches the
    logo lettering.
- **Colours sampled from the logo:** forest teal `#285F5F`, leaf lime
  `#AECD54`, sun orange `#E6883C`, white.

## Owner preferences

- Shades of **green** as the background colour and the overall tone.
- They liked these five sites (studied 2026-11-07):

The Pearl Spa is the one with the background video, close to what we built
for Elite Dental Lab. Its deep green, cream and light serif already match
the owner's request for green, so the prototype follows it most closely.

## What the prototype takes from each

- **The Pearl:** hero video, deep green and cream, Cormorant + light sans,
  "What brings you in today?" category strip.
- **Christie Adam:** offer bar, phone + Book Now in the header, one large
  featured review, photo grid.
- **Nordine:** tabbed menu with dotted lines to the price (Menu & Prices page),
  hours and map on the Visit section.
- **Craft:** a white text card overlapping a big photo (Our Story).
- **Spa in the Valley:** the band of three calls to action (Book / Gift cards /
  Walk-ins) above the footer.
