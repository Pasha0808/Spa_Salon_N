Our story

In the heart of Pikesville

Neha Salon & Spa is right in the heart of Pikesville, just half a mile south of the Beltway at 1501 Reisterstown Road. The spa offers a wide range of services for both women and men.

After careful planning and attention to every detail, the spa was formally opened in March 2013 by the Mayor of Baltimore, Stephanie Rawlings-Blake. At more than 6,000 square feet, it brings together a full hair salon and state-of-the-art treatment rooms for facials, massages and body treatments.

Whether you are coming in for a shampoo and style or a full spa package, our goal is the same: exceptional service, customized to you.

The founder

Neha Gupta

Neha Gupta has been part of the Baltimore community for over 18 years. She worked in several salons before opening her own, and is known throughout Maryland for her skill of using a single thread to create the perfect brow.

She always dreamed of opening an upscale spa that would cater to everyone: manicures and pedicures, hair styling, massage therapy, facials, body treatments, spa packages, spa parties, henna tattoos, makeup and more. Each treatment is customized to meet the client's individual needs.

It's all about feeling beautiful.
