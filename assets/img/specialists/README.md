# Sample specialist portraits

These 24 portraits are **AI-generated fictional people** for the 24 sample
profiles in `js/specialists.js`. They are not Neha Threading staff and must
not be presented as real employees. The live cards keep the visible
**Sample profile** label for that reason.

Generated on 2026-11-10, then center-cropped and optimized to 800 × 1000
WebP at quality 82. Replace them one-for-one when the salon supplies its
real team portraits.

## Prompt pattern

Each image used a role-specific variation of this prompt:

> Create a polished editorial staff portrait for a luxury day-spa website:
> a fictional [subject and salon role], warm/confident professional
> expression, elegant deep-forest or charcoal salon uniform,
> head-and-shoulders pose, soft cream-and-sage spa interior background,
> diffused window light, realistic premium-but-approachable photography,
> centered vertical 4:5 composition, no text, no logo, no watermark. This
> is a representative sample portrait, not a real person.
