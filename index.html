Our story

Known across Maryland for the perfect brow.

Neha Gupta has been part of the Baltimore community for nearly two decades, and is known throughout Maryland for her skill with a single cotton thread. She always dreamed of an upscale spa with something for everyone.

That dream became our 6,000 sq ft Pikesville spa, formally opened in March 2013 by Baltimore Mayor Stephanie Rawlings-Blake. Today our qualified, experienced team offers threading, hair, nails, massage, facials, Vichy showers, Ayurvedic face and body treatments and spa parties — each one customized to you.

— Neha Gupta, founder

Good to know

Questions, answered

Does threading hurt?

Most guests feel a quick tingle rather than pain, and it fades within minutes. Threading touches only the hair, not the skin, which makes it gentler than waxing for many people.

How long does it last?

Results typically last three to five weeks. Regular visits every three to four weeks keep your shape crisp, and over time hair can grow back finer.

Is it safe for sensitive skin?

Yes — no chemicals or hot wax touch your skin. It is a popular choice for anyone using retinol or other skin treatments that make waxing risky. Tell us about any skin concerns before we start.

How do I book?

Call Pikesville at 410-653-BROW (2769) or Canton at 410-675-BROW (2769). For massages and spa treatments, please arrive 15 minutes early to change into your robe and slippers, relax in our lobby and enjoy refreshments while you fill out any paperwork.

How should I prepare?

Come with clean, makeup-free brows if you can, and let a couple of weeks of growth come in if you want a full reshape.
