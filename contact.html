    Questions, answered

    How do I book?

    Send a request on our booking page and we will confirm by email or phone, or call us at 410-653-BROW (2769).

    When should I arrive?

    For massages and spa treatments, please arrive 15 minutes early to change into your robe and slippers, relax in our lobby and enjoy refreshments while you fill out any paperwork.

    Does threading hurt?

    Most guests feel a quick tingle rather than pain, and it fades within minutes. Threading touches only the hair, not the skin, which makes it gentler than waxing for many people.

    How long does threading last?

    Results typically last three to five weeks. Regular visits keep your shape crisp.
